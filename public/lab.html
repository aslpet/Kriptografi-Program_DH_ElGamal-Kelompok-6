B. Exponential ElGamal & Tally Homomorfik

EA Keygen x=7: h = 4^7 mod 23 = 8 Voter 1 (v=1, k=3): c1 = 4^3 mod 23 = 18, c2 = 4 · 8^3 mod 23 = 1 Voter 2 (v=0, k=5): c1 = 4^5 mod 23 = 12, c2 = 1 · 8^5 mod 23 = 16 Agregasi C1: C1 = (18 · 12) mod 23 = 216 mod 23 = 9 Agregasi C2: C2 = (1 · 16) mod 23 = 16 Dekripsi s: s = 9^7 mod 23 = 4, s^-1 mod 23 = 6 (karena 4·6 = 24 ≡ 1) Hasil g^T: g^T = (16 · 6) mod 23 = 96 mod 23 = 4 Hasil Suara T: g^T = 4 = 4^1 ⇒ T = 1 Suara (Cocok!)
